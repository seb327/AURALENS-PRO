# Privacy Policy (Draft)

Last updated: 2026-05-25

AuraLens (the "app") is operated by Vybstak. This draft policy outlines how we handle data in Phase 1.

## What we collect
- Camera scans you explicitly initiate.
- Photos you explicitly upload.
- Symbolic readings generated from those inputs.

## What we do not collect
- No background camera access.
- No biometric profile across sessions.
- No advertising identifiers.

## Where data lives
- Phase 1: encrypted local storage on your device via AsyncStorage.
- Phase 2 (opt-in): Supabase cloud sync with signed URLs and per-user RLS.

## What we never do
- Sell face data.
- Train models on your images without explicit opt-in.
- Share readings with third parties for marketing.

## Your control
- Delete every reading and image at any time from Settings → Delete My Data.
- Deletion is immediate and permanent.

## Not medical advice
Readings are symbolic reflections, not medical, psychological, or diagnostic advice. In an emergency contact your local services.
